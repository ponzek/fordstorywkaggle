Beyond Ford: Exploring Affordable Used-Car Alternatives in the United States

What are the alternatives to a used Ford Escape for a U.S. shopper on a budget? Nine connected views compare five compact SUVs (2016–2019, up to 125,000 miles, clean title) across budget fit, price spread, model years, odometer mileage, matched price gaps, U.S. Census regions, reported condition, estimated fuel cost, and actual historical listings.

Act 1: Set a budget — which compact SUVs fit?

Start with a $15,000 asking-price cap across historical U.S. Craigslist listings (historical snapshot) and watch how changing the cap unlocks non-Ford alternatives, typical prices, and model-year bands.

View 1

Budget coverage

Share of each model’s filtered listings at or below any budget cap (0–100%). Click a model tile or press Play ($10k–$20k) to see how many options open up.

What it shows

Cumulative step lines show qualifying / total filtered listings for each compact SUV as the dollar cap rises.

Second-source check

Why this form

Both counts and shares sit alongside the step lines so small groups never masquerade as large markets.

View 2

Asking-price spread

Typical (median) asking price (shape mark) and middle 50% of prices (bar) on a shared USD axis with the active budget cap. Click a model row to focus Views 4 and 6.

Show spread numbers as a table

What it shows

Where the middle half of asking prices sits for every model relative to your dashed budget marker.

How it is built

Computed from the uncapped filtered pool so raising or lowering the budget moves only the cap marker and in-budget counts.

Why this form

Medians resist high-price outlier trims while keeping listing counts (n) directly beside each brand.

View 3

Price by model year

Median asking price across 2016–2019 model years. Gaps mark years with zero matching listings. Click a year point to isolate or restore that model year.

What it shows

How it is built

Each vertex is a median in USD with consistent model colors and distinct geometric shapes.

Why this form

Aligning model years against the horizontal budget line reveals which model years cross into budget.

View 6

Regional availability

Share of listings for the focused SUV fitting the budget in each U.S. Census region, with exact qualifying / total counts. Click a bar to filter the entire story by region.

What it shows

Regional budget availability across Northeast, Midwest, South, and West plus the combined national count-summed share.

Math rule

Combined totals sum regional qualifying and total counts first before dividing—never averaging percentages blindly.

Why this matters

Used compact SUV pricing varies by climate and market supply across U.S. regions.

View 8

Fuel-cost scenario

Hypothetical annual fuel spend: (miles/yr ÷ MPG) × $/gal across 20, 25, 30 MPG benchmarks and your selected vehicle’s EPA reference MPG. Fuel only.

What it shows

Bars anchored at $0 show how annual driving and fuel economy change yearly fuel bills (e.g., 12,000 mi/yr at 25 MPG and $3.50/gal = $1,680/yr).

Assumptions labeled

Because Craigslist lacks exact drivetrain/trim on every row, benchmark bars use labeled 20/25/30 MPG assumptions and FuelEconomy.gov model defaults.

Scope limit

Estimate covers fuel cost only; insurance, repairs, financing, and taxes are excluded.
